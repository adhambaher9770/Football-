FORMULA 1

The top of all motorsport

What is Formula 1

Formula 1 is one of the hardest sports of all time. In an F1 team, it takes at least 1,300 people to make both cars. F1 cars hit terrifying speeds, and drivers can lose up to 4kg every single race. Changing normal car tires can take 10 to 15 minutes but, in F1 it takes only 2.5 seconds. The tire guns can rotate 15,000 times in a minute and the wheels utilize a single large nut to make changing them much easier. The speed limit in the pitlane is 60 km/h; at 60.1 km/h you will receive a 5-second penalty on your time. That is why they call Formula 1 the pinnacle of motorsport.

The Physics

Extreme G-Forces

At the start of the race, the driver will be hit by 2G of force, the car pulls 4G, and in corners up to 6G—imagine this! When you hit the brakes at 120 km/h, the G-force is intense.

Go-Karting Foundations

Formula 4 Progression

Steps 3 & 4

F3, F2, and the F1 Dream

Third is FORMULA 3. It's even bigger and faster, and you will stay there for 1 to 3 years. Fourth is Formula 2, where you will stay for 1 to 3 seasons. If you become a world champion, you will graduate to F1 like Lando Norris or Charles Leclerc, get into an F1 academy for 2 seasons, or someone might offer you a direct seat in F1.
